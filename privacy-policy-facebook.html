Privacy Policy for Leitores e Escritores

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Leitores e Escritores (the “App”) lets you connect your Meta accounts, read account information, and schedule posts. This policy explains how the App handles information for these features. For privacy questions, contact ed4ngelis@gmail.com.

Information collected Delete your data Contact

1. Information we process

  • Connected account information: account identifiers, names, profile information, and information about the accounts or Pages you authorize the App to access. The information available depends on the permissions you grant and the connected Meta service.
  • Authorization information: access tokens and granted permissions needed to read connected account information and publish the posts you schedule.
  • Post content and schedules: text, images, videos, links, destination accounts or Pages, scheduled publication times, and publication status associated with posts you create or schedule through the App.
  • Support communications: your email address and information you send when you contact us, including privacy and deletion requests.

Connecting an account authorizes access within the permissions you grant. Do not include sensitive personal information in post content unless you intend to publish it to the selected audience.

2. How we use information

We use connected account information to identify the accounts available to you and display them in the App. We use authorization information, post content, and scheduling details to prepare and publish posts to your chosen destinations at the times you select, and to show their publication status. We use support communications to answer questions and handle requests.

We use information received from Meta to provide these features in accordance with the permissions you grant. Revoking permissions may prevent the App from retrieving account information or publishing scheduled posts.

3. Sharing and publication

When you schedule a post, its content and the information needed to publish it are sent to the relevant Meta service. Published content is visible according to the destination account’s settings and the audience you select. Other people may view, copy, or share published content.

Service providers supporting hosting, storage, and operation of the App may process information as needed to provide those services. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or threats to people’s rights. We do not sell personal information or use Meta account data for unrelated advertising.

Meta’s handling of information through its own services is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention

We retain connected account information and authorization information while needed to maintain your connection and provide the features you request. Scheduled post content and scheduling records are retained as needed to publish posts, display their status, and resolve publication issues. Support communications are retained as needed to resolve your request.

When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we delete it or anonymize it, except where limited retention is necessary to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or address security incidents. You may request deletion at any time using the instructions below.

5. Request deletion of your data

To request deletion of information held by Leitores e Escritores, including information received from Meta:

  1. Email ed4ngelis@gmail.com with the subject “Leitores e Escritores — Data deletion request”.
  2. Include the name and profile or Page URL of the connected account so we can locate the associated information. Do not send your password or access tokens.
  3. We may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify your authority over the account. We will handle your request without undue delay and within the period required by applicable law, and confirm the outcome by email.

Deletion covers connected account information, stored authorization information, and associated scheduled post content and scheduling records held by the App, subject to the limited retention exceptions described above. Deleting this information prevents the App from completing affected scheduled posts.

Deleting data from the App does not remove posts already published on Facebook or other Meta services. Manage or delete those posts directly through the relevant service. You can also revoke the App’s access through the connected Meta account’s app or business integration settings; revoking access does not itself request deletion of information already held by us.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Send requests to ed4ngelis@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

You control which accounts you connect and which posts you schedule. You can revoke granted permissions in the relevant Meta service’s settings.

7. Security

Access tokens and unpublished post content should be treated as confidential information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your connected account or information has been compromised, revoke the App’s access and contact us.

8. Children’s privacy

Use of connected Meta accounts is subject to the relevant service’s age requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the App without authorization required by applicable law, contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App or its data practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. We will provide additional notice where required by applicable law.
