PlayLingo - Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your PlayLingo account and all associated data, please send an email request as described below.

Send your request to: play.language.info@gmail.com

Subject line: Account Deletion Request

Please include: The email address you registered with in the app.

What gets deleted

Once we verify your request, we will permanently delete the following data associated with your account from our servers:

  • Name, email address, birth date, country, and language preference
  • Challenge scores, session history, and ranking entries
  • Daily goal progress

How long it takes

Requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

Note

Deleting your account removes your data from our backend servers. Locally stored app data is removed automatically when you uninstall the app from your device.
