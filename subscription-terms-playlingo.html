PlayLanguage Premium Subscription Terms

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Premium is an optional monthly, auto-renewing Google Play subscription. The exact localized price and billing currency shown by Google Play before confirmation are the price that applies to your purchase.

Premium benefits

  • No PlayLanguage banner or interstitial ads while Premium is verified.
  • Offline entry to bundled lessons, games, and challenges for up to seven days after successful server verification, and never beyond the verified paid expiry.
  • Purchase restoration through Google Play for the same eligible Play account and PlayLanguage identity.

Rankings, goals and calendar synchronization, uncached real-time translations, purchase actions, and subscription management require an internet connection for all users.

Billing and renewal

Google Play charges the payment method associated with your Play account when you confirm the purchase and at the start of each monthly billing cycle. Premium renews automatically each month until canceled. Taxes, currency, regional availability, and the final price are determined and displayed by Google Play.

Canceling

You may cancel through Google Play subscriptions. Uninstalling PlayLanguage or deleting a PlayLanguage account does not cancel billing. Unless Google Play or applicable law provides otherwise, cancellation stops future renewal and Premium remains available through the already-paid period.

Payment problems and status changes

Access may continue during a Google Play payment grace period. Pending, paused, on-hold, expired, revoked, or unverified subscriptions do not grant Premium. A canceled subscription may continue only until its verified paid expiry. The app periodically needs internet access to refresh status; the offline snapshot expires after at most seven days.

Refunds

Purchases and refund eligibility are handled through Google Play and are subject to Google Play’s refund rules and applicable law. PlayLanguage does not collect your full payment-card details.

Availability and changes

RF Games may improve or replace Premium features while maintaining sustained subscription value. Material changes to price or billing are handled with the notices and consent required by Google Play or applicable law. Service interruptions, device restrictions, account mismatch, fraud prevention, or store/backend availability may temporarily prevent verification.
