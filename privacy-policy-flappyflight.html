Privacy Policy for Flappy Flight

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Flappy Flight ("the app") is developed by Ed Angelis ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

Flappy Flight does not require an account, login, or profile, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, or any personal contact information.

The app displays advertising through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID)
  • Approximate (coarse) location, derived from IP address
  • App activity such as ad interactions, and basic device/technical information (device model, OS version, language, network type)

We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

How this information is used

Data collected by AdMob is used to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud and abuse. This processing is performed by Google, acting as an independent controller/processor for its advertising services. You can learn more about Google's practices at Google's Partner Sites policy and manage your ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party that receives data from this app is Google (AdMob), for the purpose of displaying and measuring advertising as described above.

Children's privacy

Flappy Flight is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children. If this app's Play Store listing indicates it is designed for children, ads are configured to comply with applicable child-directed advertising treatment (e.g., Google's Families Ads Program requirements and COPPA), which limits the data collected for ad personalization accordingly.

Your choices

You can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, or opt out of interest-based advertising, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads / Google > Ads) or via Google Ad Settings. Uninstalling the app stops all data collection by it.

Data retention

We do not operate our own servers or database for this app; we do not retain any user data ourselves. Any data processed by AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
